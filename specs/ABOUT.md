App name: Radarly

Quick summary of your app
AI Tool Radar — a site that automatically discovers, scores, and showcases trending AI tools and apps. An AI agent monitors sources (Product Hunt, GitHub, HN, Reddit) daily, summarizes each tool, scores its momentum, and publishes it as a visual card. Visitors get a fast, scannable feed of "what's rising right now" in AI tooling — organized by category (coding, design, productivity, content, etc.) — without digging through forums themselves.
Audience: developers, indie hackers, builders who want to stay ahead on new AI tools
Core job of the page: let someone scan what's trending in under 10 seconds and click through to what's relevant to them
Feel: credible and current — like a signal you can trust, not a spammy content farm
